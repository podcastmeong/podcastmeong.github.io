The Patience of Prophet Ayub: Patience Without Limits

Based on the story of Prophet Ayub in the Quran · 3 min read

Prophet Ayub is the most famous example of patience. His wealth, his children, and his health were taken from him — yet Ayub stayed patient and kept praying. He never stopped remembering Allah.

The prayer of Prophet Ayub

“Indeed, adversity has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful.”

Quran 21:83

Notice his prayer: Ayub did not complain and did not get angry. He simply told Allah about his condition, then praised Allah as the Most Merciful.

Allah gave it all back

Allah answered his prayer. Allah removed his illness, returned his family to him, and gave them twice as much — as a mercy from Allah and a lesson for those who worship Him (Quran 21:84; 38:43).

Lessons for children

First, be patient in hardship. Trials can come to anyone, even to a prophet. Second, pray. When you are sick or in trouble, tell Allah about it, just like Ayub did. Third, never give up hope. Allah does not abandon His servant who returns to Him. Fourth, Allah is the Most Merciful. After the trial, Allah gives a way out.

What the Quran does not mention, we do not write: how long Ayub was ill, the name of his illness, the name of his wife, or how many children he had. What the Quran tells us is simply this: he was patient, he prayed, and Allah saved him.

Sources: Quran 38:41–44; 21:83–84; 6:84; 4:163. This article follows the eBook “Prophet Ayub and the Patience That Never Ran Out” (Children’s Prophet Stories — Dunia Kiki), whose contents are verified from the Quran.
