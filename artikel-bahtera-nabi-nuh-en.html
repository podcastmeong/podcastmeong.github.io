The Ark of Prophet Nuh: Obedient Even When Mocked

Based on the story of Prophet Nuh in the Quran · 3 min read

Prophet Nuh preached to his people for 950 years. Most of them refused to believe. Then Allah commanded him to build a great ark — on dry land, far from the sea. People mocked him.

Obedient even when mocked

Nuh kept building the ark, piece by piece, because it was Allah’s command. The mockery did not stop him. This is the first lesson of his story: obey Allah even when others laugh at you.

The great flood and the saving ark

When the great flood came, the ark saved Nuh and the believers with him, together with pairs of animals. The people who rejected his message were left behind by the flood.

Lessons for children

First, be patient in preaching. Nuh preached for hundreds of years without giving up. Second, obey even when mocked. Allah’s command is carried out, whatever people say. Third, Allah saves the believers. The ark became salvation because Nuh obeyed.

Sources: Quran 11:25–48; 29:14; 71:1–28. This article follows the eBook “Prophet Nuh and the Saving Ark” (Children’s Prophet Stories — Dunia Kiki), whose contents are verified from the Quran and authentic hadith.
