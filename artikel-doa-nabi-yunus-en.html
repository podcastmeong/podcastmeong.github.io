The Prayer of Prophet Yunus: A Prayer for Hard Times

Based on the story of Prophet Yunus in the Quran · 3 min read

Prophet Yunus was swallowed by a great fish and found himself inside its belly, in deep darkness. It was there that he prayed to Allah. His prayer was short, but beautiful — and Allah saved him.

The prayer

“There is no god but You. Glory be to You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers.”

Quran 21:87

Three things in one prayer

In this short prayer, Prophet Yunus did three things. First, he declared that there is no god but Allah. Second, he praised Allah: Glory be to You. Third, he admitted his mistake: I have been of the wrongdoers.

Admitting a mistake is a good deed. Allah loves the servant who admits their mistake and returns to Allah.

When to read this prayer

This prayer can be read when we are in hardship, when we feel surrounded by darkness, or when we realise we have done wrong. Prophet Yunus read it in the darkest place — and Allah heard him.

Allah says after that: He answered his prayer and saved him from distress. And thus do We save the believers (Quran 21:88).

A lesson for children

When life is hard, pray to Allah. Never stop remembering Allah. And never make a decision while angry — Prophet Yunus left in anger, and then he was swallowed by the great fish. Calm yourself first, then pray.

Sources: Quran 21:87–88; 37:139–148; 68:48–50. This article follows the eBook “Prophet Yunus Inside the Whale” (Children’s Prophet Stories — Dunia Kiki), whose contents are verified from the Quran.
