Caring for an Orange Cat: 5 Basics

For beginners who just adopted a cat like Oyen · 3 min read

Orange cats are famous for being chatty, clingy, and food-obsessed. Caring for one is not hard — these five basics are enough to keep them healthy and happy.

1. The right food and clean water

Feed cat food matched to their age (kitten, adult, senior), in the portions shown on the package. Do not give seasoned human food, bones, or chocolate. Clean drinking water should always be available and changed every day.

2. A clean litter box

Provide one litter box, scoop it every day, and change the litter regularly. Cats are very clean animals — a dirty litter box stresses them out and can make them go outside the box.

3. Play every day

Orange cats are active and need play. Spend 10–15 minutes a day with toys like a feather wand or a ball. Play keeps their weight healthy and stops them from getting bored.

4. Vet checkups

Take your cat to the vet for vaccinations and regular checkups, and for deworming as your vet advises. If your cat suddenly stops eating, becomes weak, or vomits repeatedly, see a vet right away — do not treat them yourself.

5. Love and a safe home

Orange cats love attention. Give them a warm bed, places to climb and hide, and do not let them roam outside unsupervised — outside there are cars, dogs, and diseases.

A general article for beginners. For health problems, always consult a veterinarian — this article is not a substitute for veterinary advice.
